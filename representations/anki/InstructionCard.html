2swap’s Connect 4

If you have somehow made it here without watching my latest Connect 4 YouTube video, reading the explanation document, and viewing the solution graph, please do! They teach the technical foundation underlying this deck.

Deck Structure

This deck is built from the solution graph and contains two parts:

  • Branches: cards teach a unique suggested move for every non-leaf node in the graph according to the weak solution.
  • Leaves: cards teach the Steady State solution for every leaf node in the graph.
  • Opening Practice: randomly shuffled opening play. Play as Red (player 1) against diverse opponents with a high coverage of the graph, until arriving at a steady state.

It's not that hard!

The whole solution graph contains ~3700 nodes. However, almost half of those are duplicates up to mirror symmetry!

This deck includes Steady State solutions for leaf nodes. Instead of memorizing those endgames, I recommend you "learn by playing." With practice, a human player can develop a pretty good intuition for endgames which renders rote memorization usually unnecessary. I have included them for completeness anyways.

Furthermore, keep your eyes out for trends and repeating patterns- these will make memorization much easier!

For new Anki users

Anki is (ironically) known for its challenging learning curve. The point of Anki is to facilitate memorizing lots of information, and it is a great tool for that job. However, Anki's high customizability makes it quite easy for the user to shoot themselves in the foot by attempting to memorize data that is poorly formatted and thereby challenging to remember, or not follow a consistent schedule.

To learn this deck, you need to do all of your reviews every day. Reviews are represented by the green numbers on the Anki home screen. If you skip a day, you will have a large backlog of reviews to catch up on. If you are overwhelmed by the number of reviews on default Anki settings, click the gear next to the "2swap's Connect 4" deck on the home screen, select "Options," and reduce the "New cards/day" setting. You should expect approximately 4 times as many reviews per day as your new cards/day setting.

Happy studying! Feel free to delete this card, as well as the Instructions subdeck.
